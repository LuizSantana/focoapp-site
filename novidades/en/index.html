What's New

What changed in each version of Foco+.

Version 3.0.0

New

  • Focus Blocker — mute distracting apps during your focus sessions (via Screen Time).
  • Ambient sounds — noise and lo-fi generated right in the app, with mixing and a sleep timer.
  • Time blocks — drag tasks onto your agenda; two-way sync with your Calendar.
  • Location reminders — get nudged when you arrive at or leave a place.
  • Advanced reports — see your focus patterns by period and category.
  • Task templates — apply sets of tasks and recurring routines in one tap.
  • Tags, search & saved filters — organize with colored tags and find anything fast.
  • Accent themes and exclusive app icons to make the app your own.
  • Audio memos and attachments on ideas — photos, files, and voice recordings.
  • Recurring reminders with an active window and occurrence count.
  • Calendar organizer and reports by category.
  • Now available in European Portuguese.
  • A fresh look and iOS 26 support.
  • Foco+ now also runs on Mac and Apple Vision Pro (one purchase).

Fixes

  • Fixed a rare crash on launch.
  • Fixed crashes when deleting tasks, ideas, reminders, and events.
  • Fixed a crash when opening Time Blocks.
  • Resolved a sync that would stall at 60%.
  • Many stability gains and layout fixes on the Today screen.

Version 2.0.1

Fixes

  • Reordering tasks by drag now works reliably and no longer conflicts with the swipe gesture.
  • Swiping a task now uses the native iOS gesture: swipe right to complete or reopen, swipe left to postpone or delete.
  • Trying to save a task or reminder without a title now shows a clear inline error and keeps your cursor in the right place.
  • Fixed an issue that prevented the App Clip from appearing via NFC, Safari or App Clip cards.
  • General stability improvements.

Version 2.0.0

New

  • Apple Watch app: see tasks, ideas and reminders, complete tasks, capture quick ideas and even run a Pomodoro right from your wrist.
  • CarPlay support: check today's tasks and reminders, complete them safely and start a Pomodoro session from your car.
  • Family Sharing: share task lists with your family through iCloud and get things done together.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: create tasks, capture ideas and set reminders by voice.
  • Focus Mode integration: customize Foco+ for each system Focus, filter urgent tasks, silence streak alerts or auto-start Pomodoro.
  • New rich text editor for Ideas: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, colors, links and photo or file attachments.
  • Backup your data: export and import everything as a .foco file or Markdown.
  • New Progress screen: weekly XP chart, history and completed task stats.
  • Share your day: export today's tasks, ideas and reminders as text to any app.
  • Foco+ arrives on Apple Vision Pro as a native app.

Fixes

  • Bug fixes and stability improvements, including more reliable notifications for repeating reminders.
